GTA CLIPS uploader

An internal publishing tool for one YouTube channel: Leonida Clips.

What it is

GTA CLIPS uploader is a private, single-operator tool. It runs on one computer owned by the channel operator and prepares short commentary videos (YouTube Shorts) about Grand Theft Auto VI live streams. It is not a public service: there are no outside users, no sign-up and no accounts other than the operator's own.

What it does

  1. Finds recent GTA VI stream recordings (VODs) from streamers who allow clipping of their streams.
  2. Picks a highlight moment and edits it into a vertical Short with original additions: an on-screen hook, a short narrated introduction giving context, word-by-word captions and a layout with the streamer's camera.
  3. Runs an automatic quality check (length, loudness, captions, no blank or frozen frames).
  4. Uploads the finished Short to the operator's own channel with the YouTube Data API (videos.insert), always as private.
  5. The operator reviews every video in YouTube Studio and decides by hand whether to publish or schedule it.

How it uses YouTube API Services

  • Only one API method: videos.insert, to upload videos to the operator's own channel.
  • Only one OAuth scope: https://www.googleapis.com/auth/youtube.upload.
  • It does not read, store, analyse or display any YouTube data, and it never accesses other users' accounts or channels.
  • At most 6 uploads per day, well within the default quota.

Credit to streamers

Every video names the streamer on screen and in its description. Only streamers who have publicly invited viewers to clip their streams are used. Any streamer can ask for their content to be removed using the contact below.
